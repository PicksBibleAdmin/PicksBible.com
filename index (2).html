Basketball Betting Glossary

  • Moneyline: Pick the winner. Overtime counts unless the market says "regulation" or "3-way".
  • Point spread: The favourite gives points and the underdog receives them. The half point (.5) removes the chance of a tie.
  • Push: On a whole-number line, landing exactly on the number returns your stake.
  • Total: Combined points scored by both teams. EuroLeague totals usually sit between 150 and 170; NBA totals between 210 and 240.
  • Player props: Bets on one player's statistics. If the player does not play, most bookmakers void the bet.

Represents Halftime + full-time result combo (e.g. 1/1, X/X, 1/2)

Betting Market Glossary

  • DNB (Draw No Bet): Removes the draw. If the match ends level, the stake is refunded.
  • HWEH (Home Team Wins Either Half): The home team must outscore the opponent in the first or the second 45 minutes.
  • AWEH (Away Team Wins Either Half): The away team must outscore the opponent in either half.
  • GG (Goal Goal / BTTS): Both teams must score at least one goal.
  • NG (No Goal): One or both teams fail to score.
  • Over/Under Goals (0.5 to 3.5): Total goals in the match. "Over 2.5" needs 3 or more goals; "Under 2.5" needs 2 or fewer.
  • HT X (Halftime Draw): The score is level at halftime.
  • HT/FT (Halftime/Full-Time): Both the halftime and full-time results must be correct (e.g. 1/1, X/1, 2/2).
