    Quick answers

    How do I activate my Gold plan?

    Open the VIP page, choose Gold and pay with your preferred method. Send your payment proof to us on WhatsApp and we will activate your plan. It then shows here as Gold VIP.

    When do VIP results appear?

    A package is shown in Results & History once all of its matches are settled. Today's picks stay private to Gold members until then.

    I forgot my password

    Go to Account Settings, open Security and choose "Email me a reset link". You can also use "Forgot password?" on the log in page.

    How do I stop receiving emails?

    Use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email we send you, or message us on WhatsApp.
