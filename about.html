About PicksBible

PicksBible is a football and basketball prediction site for bettors in Nigeria, across Africa and worldwide.

What we do

We publish free daily predictions for football and basketball: 1X2, double chance, BTTS, goals, HT/FT and correct score for football; moneyline, spreads, totals and player props for basketball.

Our premium plan adds curated 2–3 odds and 5–15 odds slips every day.

How we predict

Every game starts with data: recent form, home and away splits, goals and points per possession, injuries and rest days. Our model turns that into win probabilities, which are the percentage rings you see in every table.

An analyst then checks team news and odds before a tip is published.

We publish every result

Wins and losses both go on the Past Results page. We think that is the only honest way to run a tips site.

Bet responsibly

Predictions are information, not guarantees. Only bet money you can afford to lose. If betting stops being fun, get free support at BeGambleAware.org. PicksBible is for adults aged 18 and over.
