Football table

One row per football fixture (add fixtures in tab 1). Enter the odds for 1, X and 2, choose your Tip (1, X or 2), then your GG/NG and Over/Under 2.5 picks with their odds, add a correct-score tip, and switch the match Live. Changes save automatically.

Basketball table

One row per basketball fixture (add fixtures in tab 1, choose Basketball). Enter the odds for Home and Away, choose your Tip (1 or 2), the projected spread (e.g. -6.5 or +20.5), the point total tip (Over/Under and the line) and the projected score, then switch the game Live. Changes save automatically.

Home page boxes

Pick a date. Tick Expert tip on ONE match per sport and write the reasoning (it appears on the Expert reasoning page). Tick Bet of the day on up to FIVE matches per sport, write the pick (e.g. 1X, Over 2.5) and, after the game, set the result and score. Matches come from tab 1. Changes save automatically.
