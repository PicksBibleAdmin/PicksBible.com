Best NBA ATS Picks Today: Against the Spread Predictions

ATS means against the spread. The bookmaker gives one team a points handicap, and an ATS pick wins when that team covers the handicap, not only when it wins the game.

Our picks consider pace, injuries and rest, recent form against the spread, matchups and where the line sits compared with our own numbers.

Each selection shows the team and spread, and the confidence rating shows how strongly we rate it.

Frequently asked questions

What does ATS mean in basketball betting?

ATS means against the spread: the team must win by more than the handicap, or lose by less than it.
